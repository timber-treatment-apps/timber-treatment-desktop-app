# Privacy Policy — Timber Treatment App

**Last updated:** 29 Aug 2026

Timber Treatment App is published by Timber Treatment Apps ("we", "us").
This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## The short version

Timber Treatment App does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on
our systems. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything you
enter stays on your own device. We never see it.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no registration. We do not
collect personal data, usage data, analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, device
identifiers, location data, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no
advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics software of any kind.

## Information the app stores on your device

The records you create — fiscal years, treatment processes, treatment runs and
timber pack dimensions — are saved to a database file held in your own user
account, in the folder your operating system provides for application data.
This file never leaves your device unless you choose to move or copy it.

When you use the Export function, the app writes an Excel (.xlsx) workbook to
the location you select in the standard save dialog. That file is yours; it is
not sent anywhere.

The app is designed for business records about timber and treatment processes
and provides no fields intended for personal information. If you choose to type
personal information into a free-text field, such as a treatment process
description, it is stored locally in the same way as all other data and is
still never transmitted to us.

## Network connections

Timber Treatment App makes no network connections of its own. It works fully
offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

The app will open your default web browser only when you deliberately choose an
action that requires it — for example, selecting a Help menu item, or using the
button on the Updates screen that opens the app's store listing. In those cases
your browser or the app store handles the connection, subject to its own
privacy terms.

## Updates

App updates are delivered and managed by the app store you installed the app
from. Any information collected during that process is handled by that store's
operator under its own privacy terms — for example, the Microsoft Privacy
Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) or the Apple Privacy
Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) — and not by us.

## Sharing and disclosure

Because we do not collect or receive any of your information, we have nothing
to share, sell, disclose, or transfer to any third party, and no data is
transferred outside your own device or to any other country.

## Your rights and control over your data

You control your data entirely. You can amend or delete any record from within
the app at any time. You may copy or back up the database file yourself.
Uninstalling the app removes its stored data from your device.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is no information for us to
provide, correct, or erase on request. If you have questions about how the app
handles data, contact us using the details below.

## Children

The app is a business record-keeping tool. It is not directed at children and
does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Security

Your records are stored within your own user account and are protected by the
security of your device and that account. We recommend keeping your device and
user account secured, and backing up the database file or your Excel exports as
part of your normal business backup routine.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted
at this address with a revised "Last updated" date.
