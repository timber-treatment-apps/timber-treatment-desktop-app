## About

**Timber Treatment App** is a desktop application for managing the chemical treatment of
timber. It gives producers and processors a single place to record the treatments they
apply - the treatment type and chemical used, the batch or load, the date applied and who
applied it - so every piece of treated timber has an accurate, searchable history behind it.

Instead of relying on paper logs or scattered spreadsheets, your treatment records are kept
consistent and easy to retrieve.

## Audit & compliance

Because every treatment is captured with its type, date and details, the app builds a
verifiable trail of **what was treated, with what, and when**. When an audit or
inspection comes around, that history is ready to hand: you can quickly demonstrate that the
correct treatments were applied to the correct stock, and answer auditor queries without
digging through old paperwork.
